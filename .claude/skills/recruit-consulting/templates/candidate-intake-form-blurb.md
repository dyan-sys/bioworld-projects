# Candidate Intake Form Blurb — Template

This is the default Ally intake form blurb. When adapting for a client role, replace the placeholders and tailor the messaging to the specific company and role.

---

## Default (Ally-branded)

Hello!

Thank you for your interest in joining the team!

Ally is an AI-native Virtual Assistant Agency that helps founders and operators build leverage in the AI era. We pair high-agency Executive Partners with great clients.

We are growing quickly and looking for ambitious individuals to join the team!

To get started with your application, please fill in this form. You will also be asked to share your updated CV, as well as attach a short video (1–2 min) introducing yourself and why you're a great fit. We suggest using Loom or a similar tool. (It's free!)

We appreciate your time and we look forward to getting to know you.

Ally Recruitment Team

---

## Adaptation Guide

When adapting for a client role:

1. **Replace company identity** — Swap "Ally" references with the client's company name and branding
2. **Name the role** — Add the specific role title (bold)
3. **One-line company positioning** — What the company does, in one sentence (use the improved JD as source)
4. **Brief role hook** — One sentence that signals what kind of candidate they're looking for (pull from the JD's opening hook or "About the Role" section)
5. **Keep the form/CV/video instructions** — These are standard and should stay as-is
6. **Sign-off** — Use the client's company name (e.g., "Hello Nancy Recruitment Team")
