## For AI Agent Execution
### Ally Recruiting - January 2026

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This document provides detailed instructions for AI agents to screen Executive Partner candidates using a systematic, data-driven scoring methodology. Candidates receive a full score (0-100) based on all six buckets. Threshold failures are flagged separately as risk factors in the recommendation, rather than automatically disqualifying candidates.

**CRITICAL AGE REQUIREMENT:** All candidates estimated to be >36 years old (as of 2026) must have their recommendation capped at "PROCEED WITH CAUTION" regardless of other strengths. Age must be estimated from graduation dates or stated age, and the override must be explicitly mentioned in the rationale.

## PART 1: SCORING FRAMEWORK OVERVIEW

### Six Strategic Buckets (Weighted)

| Bucket | Weight | Threshold | Purpose |
|--------|--------|-----------|---------|
| Education | 20% | None | Assess academic foundation and rigor |
| Experience & Trajectory | 20% | ≥10.0 raw | Evaluate depth, growth, and high-pressure environment |
| Skills | 20% | ≥5.0 raw | Assess operational, strategic, and core VA capabilities |
| Communication | 20% | ≥2.0 raw | Evaluate written clarity and resume presentation quality |
| Context Knowledge | 10% | None | Bonus for target industry experience |
| Other Factors | 10% | None | Kicker: age, awards, learning, references |

### Final Score Calculation

```
Final Score (0-100) = [
  (Education_Equiv_0-4 × 0.20) +
  (Experience_Equiv_0-4 × 0.20) +
  (Skills_Equiv_0-4 × 0.20) +
  (Communication_Equiv_0-4 × 0.20) +
  (Context_Equiv_0-4 × 0.10) +
  (Other_Equiv_0-4 × 0.10)
] × 25
```

Where each "Equiv_0-4" is the equivalent 0-4 value looked up from the interpretation tables.

### Tier Interpretation (Final Score)

| Score | Tier | Base Recommendation |
|-------|------|-----------------|
| 70-100 | Tier 1 Strong | STRONG PROCEED - Schedule interview immediately |
| 60-69 | Tier 1 Strong / Tier 2 Viable | PROCEED - Schedule interview |
| 50-59 | Tier 2 Viable / Marginal | PROCEED WITH QUESTIONS - Interview if capacity |
| 40-49 | Tier 3 Below Threshold | PROCEED WITH CAUTION - Only if others unavailable |
| <40 | Tier 3 Reject | DO NOT PROCEED - Significant gaps |

### Threshold Assessment & Recommendation Adjustment

**Three Core Thresholds (Flagged, Not Disqualifying):**
- Experience & Trajectory ≥ 10.0 raw (MEETS level)
- Skills ≥ 5.0 raw (MEETS level)
- Communication ≥ 2.0 raw (MEETS level)

**Recommendation Logic:**

| Score | Thresholds Passed | Recommendation |
|-------|---|---|
| 70-100 | 3/3 | **STRONG PROCEED** - Schedule interview immediately |
| 70-100 | 2/3 | **PROCEED** - Schedule interview; note threshold gap |
| 70-100 | 1/3 or 0/3 | **PROCEED WITH QUESTIONS** - Interview to assess gap |
| 60-69 | 3/3 | **PROCEED** - Schedule interview |
| 60-69 | 2/3 | **PROCEED WITH QUESTIONS** - Interview; note threshold gap |
| 60-69 | 1/3 or 0/3 | **PROCEED WITH CAUTION** - Interview only if strong need |
| 50-59 | 3/3 | **PROCEED WITH QUESTIONS** - Interview if capacity |
| 50-59 | 2/3 | **PROCEED WITH CAUTION** - Interview only if strong need |
| 50-59 | 1/3 or 0/3 | **DO NOT PROCEED** - Too many gaps; only interview if desperate |
| 40-49 | Any | **DO NOT PROCEED** - Below threshold; only if others unavailable |

## BUCKET 2: EXPERIENCE & TRAJECTORY (20% weight)

### Purpose
Evaluate the depth, quality, and progression of the candidate's professional experience. This bucket assesses whether the candidate has worked in high-pressure environments, demonstrated growth, and developed strategic EA capabilities.

**Critical Question:** Can this person perform under pressure and was this person trusted in a high-pressure environment?

### Important Note on Company Research
**For 2.2 (Company Tier & Rigor), if it is not immediately clear what tier the company is, you MUST conduct research on the actual company to make this assessment.** Do not guess. Research the company size, industry, growth stage, and reputation.

### Scoring Breakdown

#### 2.1 Total EA Experience (Raw Points: 0-3)

| Years | Points |
|-------|--------|
| 5+ years | 3 |
| 3-5 years | 2 |
| 2-3 years | 1.5 |
| 1-2 years | 1 |
| <1 year | 0 |

**Scoring Rule:** Count only roles explicitly labeled as EA, Executive Assistant, Executive Partner, or equivalent. Do not count general administrative roles.

#### 2.2 Company Tier & Rigor Assessment (Raw Points: 0-3)

**IMPORTANT:** If company tier is not immediately clear, research the company.

**Tier A - High Rigor (3 points):**
- Fortune 500 companies
- Tier A venture-backed startups (Series B+ with $10M+ funding or clear market traction)
- Multinational corporations
- Large tech companies (Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, Apple, etc.)
- C-Suite executive support (CEO, President, COO, CFO)
- Fast-paced, high-stakes environments
- Evidence: Supporting demanding executives, complex decision-making, high stakes

**Tier B - Medium Rigor (2 points):**
- Mid-size companies (100-1000 employees)
- Series A startups or well-funded pre-Series A
- Regional leaders in industry
- Mid-level executive support (VP, Director, General Manager)
- Moderate pace, some complexity
- Evidence: Moderate complexity, standard business demands

**Tier C - Lower Rigor (1 point):**
- Small companies (<100 employees)
- Non-profit organizations
- Government agencies
- Lower-level manager support
- Routine tasks, predictable workflows
- Evidence: Limited complexity, routine administrative work

**Scoring Rule:** Assess the company AND the executive supported. Supporting a CEO at Fortune 500 = 3. Supporting a manager at a small company = 1.

#### 2.3 Scope & Complexity of Role (Raw Points: 0-3)

| Score | Definition |
|-------|-----------|
| **3** | High scope: Multi-function support, budget management, strategic projects, cross-team coordination, business operations |
| **2** | Medium scope: Calendar, inbox, events, some project support, coordination across 2-3 functions |
| **1** | Limited scope: Primarily calendar/inbox, basic scheduling, routine admin |
| **0** | No EA experience or purely transactional |

**Scoring Rule:** Look for evidence of breadth and complexity in role descriptions.

#### 2.4 Trajectory & Growth (Raw Points: 0-3)

| Score | Definition |
|-------|-----------|
| **3** | Clear promotion trajectory: EA → Senior EA → Operations role; OR rapid advancement with increasing responsibility |
| **2** | Some growth: Expanded scope, additional responsibilities, lateral move to better company |
| **1** | Stable but limited growth: Same role for 2+ years with no advancement |
| **0** | Job-hopping or declining trajectory: Multiple short roles, moving to lower-tier companies |

**Scoring Rule:** Assess whether candidate is demonstrating growth and ambition.

#### 2.4a Depth of EA Responsibilities (Raw Points: 0-3)

| Score | Definition |
|-------|-----------|
| **3** | Strategic EA work: Process improvement, systems creation, business operations, stakeholder management, strategic projects; Evidence of thought partnership with executive |
| **2** | Mixed responsibilities: Some strategic work + routine admin; Calendar/inbox + some projects |
| **1** | Transactional VA work: Primarily calendar, inbox, data entry, scheduling; Limited strategic involvement |
| **0** | No EA experience; Freelance data entry or purely administrative roles |

**Scoring Rule:** Read role descriptions carefully. Look for evidence of strategic thinking, not just task completion.

#### 2.4b Embedded vs. Freelance/Remote Context (Raw Points: 0-2)

| Score | Definition |
|-------|-----------|
| **2** | Embedded in organization: In-office or long-term remote role (2+ years); Deep relationships; Part of executive's daily operations; Understood business context |
| **1** | Mixed context: Some embedded experience + some freelance; Moderate tenure (1-2 years) |
| **0** | Primarily freelance/contract: Short-term engagements; Remote data work; Limited organizational integration; "Available upon request" references |

**Scoring Rule:** Assess tenure and organizational integration.

#### 2.4c Consistency & Deepening of Expertise (Raw Points: 0-2)

| Score | Definition |
|-------|-----------|
| **2** | Clear specialization: Stayed in EA roles and deepened expertise; Promoted or expanded scope; Consistent focus on executive support |
| **1** | Some consistency: Mix of EA and related roles; Some progression but also lateral moves |
| **0** | Job-hopping or role-switching: Multiple different types of roles; Less than 1 year per role; No clear specialization |

**Scoring Rule:** Look at career progression. Multiple EA roles with increasing scope = 2. Mix of EA, content writing, data entry, customer service = 0.

## BUCKET 6: OTHER FACTORS (10% weight)

### Purpose
Kicker factors that can differentiate candidates at the margins.

### Age Estimation Guidelines

**IMPORTANT:** Age alignment is critical for this role. You MUST estimate candidate age and apply the override rule in Part 3.

**Current Year for All Calculations: 2026**

#### Method 1: Bachelor's Degree Graduation Year
```
Estimated Age = 22 + (2026 - Graduation Year)
```

**Examples:**
- Graduated 2012 → 22 + (2026 - 2012) = 22 + 14 = **36 years old** ✓ Acceptable
- Graduated 2010 → 22 + (2026 - 2010) = 22 + 16 = **38 years old** ✋ Age Override Applies
- Graduated 2014 → 22 + (2026 - 2014) = 22 + 12 = **34 years old** ✓ Acceptable

#### Method 2: Master's/MBA Graduation Year
```
Estimated Age = 24 + (2026 - Graduation Year)
```
Assume completion at age 24 (typical for 2-year Master's after Bachelor's at 22).

**Examples:**
- MBA graduated 2015 → 24 + (2026 - 2015) = 24 + 11 = **35 years old** ✓ Acceptable
- MBA graduated 2012 → 24 + (2026 - 2012) = 24 + 14 = **38 years old** ✋ Age Override Applies

#### Method 3: Explicitly Stated Age
If the candidate states their age directly on the resume:
```
Estimated Age (as of 2026) = Stated Age + Years Since Resume Date
```

**Example:**
- Resume states "28 years old" dated January 2024 → 28 + 2 = **30 years old** ✓ Acceptable
- Resume states "35 years old" dated January 2024 → 35 + 2 = **37 years old** ✋ Age Override Applies

#### Method 4: Work Experience Timeline
If no graduation date or stated age, estimate conservatively based on first job year:
```
Estimated Age = 22 + (2026 - First Job Year)
```

**Scoring Rule:** Use the most reliable method available. If multiple signals conflict, note the discrepancy in the rationale.

## PART 3: FINAL SCORING & RECOMMENDATION

### Step 1: Calculate All Six Bucket Raw Scores

2. **Experience & Trajectory:** 0-19 raw
3. **Skills:** 0-14 raw
4. **Communication:** 0-4 raw
5. **Context Knowledge:** 0-4 raw
6. **Other Factors:** 0-8 raw

### Step 2: Convert to Equivalent 0-4 Values

For each bucket, look up the raw score in the interpretation table and get the **Equivalent 0-4** value:

- Education: Look up raw score → get Equivalent 0-4
- Experience & Trajectory: Look up raw score → get Equivalent 0-4
- Skills: Look up raw score → get Equivalent 0-4
- Communication: Look up raw score → get Equivalent 0-4
- Context Knowledge: Look up raw score → get Equivalent 0-4

### Step 3: Calculate Final Score (0-100)

```
Final Score = [
  (Education_Equiv_0-4 × 0.20) +
  (Experience_Equiv_0-4 × 0.20) +
  (Skills_Equiv_0-4 × 0.20) +
  (Communication_Equiv_0-4 × 0.20) +
```

### Step 4: Assess Threshold Status

### Step 5: Assign Tier & Recommendation

Use the recommendation logic table from Part 1 to determine recommendation based on:
1. Final Score (0-100)
2. Number of thresholds passed (0/3, 1/3, 2/3, or 3/3)

### Step 5a: Apply Age Override (CRITICAL)

**Age Override Rule:**

If the candidate's estimated age > 36 years (as of 2026), apply the following override:

```
IF Estimated Age > 36:
  IF Base Recommendation is "STRONG PROCEED" OR "PROCEED" OR "PROCEED WITH QUESTIONS":
    → DOWNGRADE to "PROCEED WITH CAUTION"
    → MUST explicitly state age override in rationale

  IF Base Recommendation is "PROCEED WITH CAUTION" OR "DO NOT PROCEED":
    → KEEP original recommendation (already appropriately cautious)
    → MUST mention age factor in rationale
```

**Rationale Requirement:**

When age override applies, your rationale MUST include explicit language such as:
- "While other factors are strong (score X/100), estimated age of Y (>36) caps recommendation at PROCEED WITH CAUTION per age alignment requirements."
- "Candidate scores well on experience and skills, but estimated age of Y based on [graduation year/stated age] triggers age override to PROCEED WITH CAUTION."

**No Age Information Available:**

If you cannot estimate age from any available information:
- Note in rationale: "Unable to estimate age - no graduation date or age information provided."
- Do NOT apply age override
- Proceed with base recommendation

## PART 4: COMMON SCORING PITFALLS TO AVOID

### Pitfall 1: Hallucinating Experience
**Problem:** Resume title says "Executive Assistant" but work history shows only engineering/testing roles.
**Solution:** Score based on ACTUAL work experience, not job title. If no EA experience, score 0 for 2.1 (Total EA Experience).

### Pitfall 2: Overweighting Tool Familiarity
**Problem:** Resume lists 40+ tools but no evidence of actual implementation or mastery.
**Solution:** Score based on depth of tool use and business impact, not breadth of tools listed. Look for specific examples.

### Pitfall 3: Missing Company Research
**Problem:** Company name is unfamiliar; random tier is assigned.
**Solution:** RESEARCH the company. Look up company size, funding stage, industry reputation. Use LinkedIn, Crunchbase, Google for research.

### Pitfall 4: Ignoring Job-Hopping Pattern
**Problem:** Multiple 6-12 month roles across different industries.
**Solution:** Use sub-criterion 2.4c (Consistency & Deepening) to flag lack of specialization. Score 0.

### Pitfall 5: Giving Credit for Unrelated Experience
**Problem:** Resume shows 10 years of customer service, labeled as "EA experience."
**Solution:** Only count roles explicitly labeled as EA, Executive Assistant, or equivalent. Customer service is not EA experience.

### Pitfall 6: Underweighting Communication
**Problem:** Candidate has great experience but sparse, unprofessional resume; this is overlooked.
**Solution:** Communication is 20% weight AND a minimum threshold. Poor communication is a critical gap for EA role.

### Pitfall 7: Forgetting Age Override
**Problem:** Candidate scores 85/100 with excellent experience but is estimated to be 38 years old; age override is not applied.
**Solution:** ALWAYS estimate age using graduation year or stated age. If age >36, cap recommendation at PROCEED WITH CAUTION and explicitly state this in the rationale. Age alignment is critical for this role.
