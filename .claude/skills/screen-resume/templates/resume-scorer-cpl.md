# Resume Scoring Rubric: Client Partnership Lead (CPL)

## Role Context

The **Client Partnership Lead (CPL)** is a leadership-track role focused on **coaching Executive Partners (EAs)** and managing client relationships in the **EA/staffing agency context**. This is NOT a traditional account manager, IT operations, or generic people management role.

**Mission:** Ensure clients get real leverage and EPs grow into world-class operators. Bridge between clients, EPs, and operations—coaching EPs, managing quality, resolving issues, guiding expectations.

**Critical context requirement:** EA/staffing agency operations experience is ESSENTIAL. IT project management, software development teams, or generic BPO operations are NOT the right background.

**Ideal backgrounds:**
- **Athena PM/OM** (Program Manager / Operations Manager)
- **Senior EA or EP** transitioned into coaching/management roles
- **Chief-of-Staff** at EA agencies or client success teams
- **Agency team leads** coaching EAs/operators and managing client partnerships

**Key success factors:**
- **EA/staffing agency experience** (Athena, Boldly, Belay, etc.) - NOT tech/IT companies
- **Coaching EAs/EPs specifically** - NOT generic team management
- **Client partnership in staffing context** - matching EPs to clients, managing EP performance
- **Quality systems for EA service delivery** - NOT software development or IT operations

## Scoring Structure

### Overview
- **6 Buckets** with different raw max scores
- **Each bucket** converts to a 0-4 equivalent scale
- **Final Score** = (Sum of equivalent scores) ÷ 6 × 25 = Score out of 100

### Buckets & Weights
1. **Education** (max 6 raw → 0-4 equivalent): 16.67%
2. **Experience Trajectory** (max 19 raw → 0-4 equivalent): 16.67%
3. **Skills** (max 16 raw → 0-4 equivalent): 16.67%
4. **Communication** (max 4 raw → 0-4 equivalent): 16.67%
5. **Context Knowledge** (max 4 raw → 0-4 equivalent): 16.67%
6. **Other Factors** (max 4 raw → 0-4 equivalent): 16.67%

## CRITICAL THRESHOLDS (Must Pass to Proceed)

Before calculating final score, verify these three thresholds:

1. **Experience Threshold**: Raw Experience score ≥ 10.0
   - Ensures sufficient people management and operations experience

2. **Skills Threshold**: Raw Skills score ≥ 8.0
   - Ensures core client partnership and coaching competencies

3. **Communication Threshold**: Raw Communication score ≥ 2.0

**If ANY threshold is not met:** Candidate receives "DO NOT PROCEED" regardless of total score.

## 2. EXPERIENCE TRAJECTORY (Max 19 raw → 0-4 equivalent)

### 2.1 Total Years of Relevant Experience (0-11 points, 1 point per year, capped at 11)

Count only **EA/staffing agency operations, client success for EA services, or EA/EP coaching roles**:
- **Operations Manager, Program Manager at EA agencies** (Athena, Boldly, Belay, etc.)
- **Team Leader, Account Manager at EA-focused BPO** (TaskUs, Concentrix IF in EA operations)
- **Senior EA/EP with coaching responsibilities** (coaching other EAs/operators)
- **Chief-of-Staff supporting founders** (with team management and EA coordination)

**Do NOT count:**
- **IT project management, software development, technical operations** - WRONG CONTEXT
- **Manufacturing, supply chain, back-office operations** - NOT relevant
- **Generic customer service or call center** without EA/operations management
- **Individual contributor EA/VA roles** without coaching or management responsibilities

**Scoring:**
- 0-2 years: 0-2 points (below minimum requirement)
- 3-5 years: 3-5 points (meets requirement)
- 6-8 years: 6-8 points (strong)
- 9-11+ years: 9-11 points (exceptional, capped at 11)

**Example:**
- 11 years as Operations Manager at Athena + TaskUs = 11 points ✅
- 11 years as IT Project Manager at IBM + TCS = 0 points ❌ (wrong context)

### 2.2 People Management Depth - EA/EP Coaching Focus (0-3 points)

**CRITICAL: Must be coaching EAs/EPs/operators in staffing/client success context, NOT generic team management or IT teams.**

- **Exceptional (3 points):**
  - Managed 100+ EA/operators OR 8+ Team Leaders coaching EA/service delivery teams
  - Multi-tiered management in EA agency (e.g., "managed 150+ teammates and 8-10 Team Leaders")
  - Demonstrated coaching EAs/EPs specifically with performance management and quality improvement
  - Evidence of developing EAs/operators into higher-performing roles

- **Strong (2 points):**
  - Managed 30-99 EAs/operators OR 3-7 direct reports coaching service teams
  - Regular 1-on-1s, performance reviews, coaching sessions with EAs/operators
  - Evidence of quality improvements in EA service delivery

- **Moderate (1 point):**
  - Managed 10-29 operators OR 1-2 direct reports in client-facing roles
  - Some coaching or mentoring of service delivery team members
  - Limited scope but in the right context (EA/client success)

- **None (0 points):**
  - No direct reports or team management
  - Managed IT/tech teams (wrong context)
  - Individual contributor role

### 2.3 Company Tier & Rigor (0-3 points)

**CRITICAL: Only EA/staffing agency operations count. Tech companies, IT consulting, and software development firms are NOT the right context for this role.**

**Tier 1 (3 points) - EA/Staffing Agencies ONLY:**
- **Premium EA agencies:** Athena, Boldly, Belay, Double, Fancy Hands, Time Etc
- **EA-focused operations:** Must have been in EA/EP operations or Program/Operations Manager role
- **EXCLUDE:** Tech companies (IBM, TCS, Accenture, Google, etc.) even if Tier 1 brand
- **EXCLUDE:** IT consulting, software development, tech project management

**Tier 2 (2 points) - BPO/Staffing with EA/Operations Focus:**
- **TaskUs, Concentrix, TTEC:** ONLY if in EA operations, client success, or program management roles (NOT IT support or tech projects)
- **Sutherland, Teleperformance, Alorica:** IF in operations management coaching service delivery teams
- **Staffing/recruitment agencies:** If managing client-facing operators

**Tier 3 (1 point) - Adjacent Operations:**
- **Chief-of-Staff roles:** Supporting founders/executives in e-commerce or startups
- **Client success operations:** SaaS companies managing high-touch client relationships
- **Small EA agencies:** <50 people but in the right context

**Tier 4 (0 points) - WRONG CONTEXT:**
- **IT/Tech operations:** Software development, technical project management, IT support
- **Manufacturing/supply chain:** Operations not in service delivery context
- **Generic BPO:** Call center, data entry, back-office without EA/client success focus
- **Self-employed/freelance VA:** No exposure to structured EA agency operations

**Bonus:** If candidate has worked at multiple Tier 1 EA agencies: +0.5 (capped at 3 total)

**CRITICAL DISTINCTION:** Operations Manager at Athena (Tier 1, EA context) ≠ Operations Manager at IBM (Tier 4, IT context)

### 2.4 Scope & Complexity of Role (0-2 points)

**High Complexity (2 points):**
- Multi-client operations management (5+ concurrent clients)
- Cross-functional coordination (finance, HR, product, etc.)
- Strategic projects requiring executive stakeholder management
- P&L responsibility or budget ownership

**Moderate Complexity (1 point):**
- Single client/program management
- Standard operations oversight
- Some cross-functional work

**Low Complexity (0 points):**
- Task execution without strategic oversight
- Single-function role
- Limited stakeholder interaction

## EVALUATION PRINCIPLES

1. **EA/Staffing Agency Context is MANDATORY**: This role requires EA agency operations experience. IT project management, software development, tech consulting, or generic BPO operations are NOT the right background—no matter how prestigious the company.

2. **Context > Brand Name**: Operations Manager at Athena (EA agency) >>> Operations Manager at IBM (tech company). Brand name only matters if it's in the EA/staffing context.

3. **Coaching EAs/EPs Specifically**: Coaching software developers, IT teams, or manufacturing staff is NOT relevant. Must have coached EAs, Executive Partners, or operators in service delivery roles.

4. **Client Partnership in Staffing Context**: Managing relationships where you're matching EPs to clients and coaching EP performance is the core role. IT client relationships (implementing software, managing tech projects) are different contexts.

5. **Distinguish Operations Contexts**:
   - ✅ **RIGHT CONTEXT**: Athena PM/OM, TaskUs operations (if EA-focused), Chief-of-Staff, senior EA coaching others
   - ❌ **WRONG CONTEXT**: IT project manager, software team lead, tech operations, manufacturing operations

6. **Be Calibrated**: Target score of ~80-85 for Operations Manager with 10+ years at Athena/Boldly/Belay, managing 50+ EAs/operators, coaching EP performance, and handling multi-client programs.

   - Shariebel (84.03): Operations Manager at Athena + TaskUs (EA/BPO context), 150+ team, 15 client programs - IDEAL ✅
   - Tech operations managers (even at IBM/Google) should score significantly lower due to context mismatch
