# Bioworld Ventures — LinkedIn Content Strategy

## Who We Are
Bioworld Ventures is a company-building studio partnering with seed-to-early-stage life sciences disruptors. Our team comprises healthcare veterans and ex-C-level executives from Johnson & Johnson, Boston Scientific, Genesis Medtech, Stryker, and Boston Consulting Group. We operate through three divisions: Capital, Labs, and Advisory.

## LinkedIn Presence
- **Page:** Bioworld Ventures (company page)
- **Followers:** ~350
- **Posting cadence:** 1 post per week (Tuesdays)
- **Primary content:** Portfolio company milestones, reposts, industry commentary

## Target Audience
1. **Primary:** Life science investors, VCs, family offices, institutional LPs
2. **Secondary:** Portfolio company stakeholders, potential co-investors
3. **Tertiary:** Biotech/medtech founders seeking strategic partners

## What Our Audience Cares About
- Portfolio performance signals (FDA clearances, funding rounds, partnerships)
- Market-moving biotech/medtech developments
- Bioworld's investment thesis and operational approach
- Clinical milestones and regulatory progress
- Industry trends that validate our portfolio positioning

## Voice & Tone
- **Professional but celebratory** — we're proud of our portfolio companies
- **Forward-looking** — emphasize impact and what comes next
- **Substantive** — lead with the news, not the hype
- **Concise** — 3-4 short paragraphs max
- **Human** — not robotic press releases, but not overly casual either

## Post Format
1. **Lead emoji + hook** — e.g., "Exciting news from our portfolio company @CorVista Health!"
2. **What happened** — the news in 1-2 sentences
3. **Why it matters** — clinical/market impact in 1-2 sentences
4. **Hashtags** — 3-5 relevant tags at the end

## Emoji Usage
Use sparingly but consistently:
- Milestone/growth news
- Applause/celebration
- Science/research
- Innovation/ideas
- Regulatory/official

## Hashtag Strategy
Always include: #PortfolioHighlight (for portfolio news)
Rotate based on content: #MedTech #BiopharmaInnovation #Innovation #DigitalHealth #Diagnostics #FDACleared #FundingRound #ClinicalTrials #LifeSciences #HealthTech

## Content Types (by priority)
1. **Regulatory milestones** — FDA clearances, breakthrough designations, CE marks
2. **Funding rounds** — Series A/B/C, IPOs, SPAC deals
3. **Partnerships & acquisitions** — strategic collaborations, M&A
4. **Executive appointments** — new C-suite, board additions
5. **Conference appearances** — LSI, JPM, BIO, MEDICA
6. **Industry trends** — broader biotech/medtech commentary with Bioworld lens
7. **Awards & recognition** — TIME lists, innovator awards

## What to Avoid
- Self-congratulatory language without substance
- Generic biotech buzzwords without specifics
- Posts that are too long (keep under 1300 characters)
- Overly technical jargon that excludes non-specialist investors
- Marketing CTAs ("DM us", "Link in bio")
- Negative commentary about competitors or industry players

## Approval Workflow
1. AI discovers news and generates draft
2. Draft posted to Notion for review
3. Ivan reviews and approves/rejects in Notion
4. Approved posts auto-publish on Tuesdays at 10 AM HKT
