# Executive Partner — Internal Posting

---

## Role Summary

Executive Partner position — internal referral channel.

## Key Responsibilities

- Manage executive calendars, scheduling, and travel arrangements
- Handle confidential correspondence and communications
- Coordinate cross-functional projects and follow-ups
- Prepare reports, presentations, and meeting materials
- Act as a liaison between executives and internal/external stakeholders

## Ideal Candidate

- 3+ years of executive assistant or chief of staff experience
- Excellent written and verbal English communication
- Strong organizational and project management skills
- Proficiency with productivity tools (Google Workspace, Notion, Slack)
- Ability to work independently in a remote environment
